# CART-2: Add and remove cart items

## Understanding

A shopper adds items to a cart and removes them. Personas: none

## Files to change

- `src/cart.py`

## Approach

A `Cart` class keeps a quantity for each item name.

## Acceptance criteria coverage

1. Add an item: `cart.R1`. Verified by a unit test.
2. Remove an item: `cart.R2`. Verified by a unit test.

## Out of scope and risks

Prices and stock are out of scope.
