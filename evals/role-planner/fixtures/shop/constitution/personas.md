# Personas

## Shopper

- id: shopper
- who: A person who buys pens and paper on the shop site, about once a month.
- jobs:
  - shopper.J1: When I find an item, I want to put it in my cart, so that I buy all items in one order.
  - shopper.J2: When I am not sure about an item, I want to keep it out of my order, so that I decide later.
  - shopper.J3: When I check out, I want to see the correct total, so that I know what I pay.
  - shopper.J4: When I get my order, I want a clear receipt, so that I can check each item.
- limits:
  - device: phone or laptop
  - skill: no technical knowledge
  - access: own account only
- evidence: assumed
